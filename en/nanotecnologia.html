Nanotechnology: from science to industry

Why nanotechnology?

Nanotechnology makes it possible to develop materials with new properties and better performance. In industry, this can mean lighter, stronger and more durable products, new functionalities and more efficient use of resources.

The scale

Between 1 and 100 nanometers, matter changes its behavior.

These possibilities come from working with structures at the nanoscale, usually between 1 and 100 nanometers. At this scale, materials can show properties that differ from those seen at larger sizes.

1 nanometer = one billionth of a meter.

Benefits for new products

Small particles. Big gains.

Higher performance

Lighter, stronger and more durable materials, tailored to the requirements of each application.

New functionalities

Self-cleaning surfaces, antimicrobial materials and coatings that repel water or provide extra protection.

Efficiency and sustainability

The opportunity to reduce waste, optimize the use of raw materials and energy and extend the service life of products.

Health innovation

Development of controlled drug-release systems and new diagnostic tools.

Where can nanotechnology be applied?

The possibilities span many industries.

The choice of materials and technologies depends on the desired properties and on the conditions of use of each product or process.

  • 01
    Agriculture and livestockControlled-release fertilizers and crop protection, additives and sensors for monitoring soil and crops.
  • 02
    Paints and coatingsProtection against corrosion, UV rays and wear, as well as self-cleaning, antimicrobial or water-repellent surfaces.
  • 03
    Roads and infrastructureDevelopment of stronger, more durable materials.
  • 04
    Health and foodActive-ingredient release systems, diagnostic technologies and packaging that helps preserve food.
  • 05
    Electronics and energyNanomaterials for batteries, energy storage and more efficient components.
  • 06
    Advanced materialsPolymers, metal alloys and composites with enhanced properties.
  • 07
    Defense and securityLight, strong structural materials, technical fabrics and high-sensitivity sensors.
From science to industry

Turning these possibilities into products requires development, testing and performance evaluation under real conditions of use.
