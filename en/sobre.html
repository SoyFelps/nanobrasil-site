About us

NanoBrasil is Latin America's nanotechnology hub, dedicated to identifying and incorporating solutions and opportunities applicable to the sector.

The company is led by entrepreneurial founding partners, focused on connecting advances already developed around the world with the needs of the Brazilian market.

Based at the Belo Horizonte Technology Park (BH-TEC), it has a laboratory equipped with cutting-edge technology for development, application and partner training.

It brings together a specialized technical team and is planning to build its own nanoparticle and nanotechnology solutions factory.

Nanotechnology at industrial scale · Custom solutions

Every industry has a specific challenge, and that is exactly where nanotechnology creates real value.

At NanoBrasil, we develop tailor-made solutions for concrete production needs, working side by side with companies to increase efficiency, reduce costs and raise the performance of products and processes. Applying nanoparticles makes it possible, for example, to improve material strength, optimize formulations and extend durability without the need for complex structural changes.

More than technology, we deliver a competitive edge: solutions designed to scale, with a direct impact on our partners' productivity and innovation.

Nanotechnology and ESG

Talking about nanotechnology also means talking about applied sustainability.

Producing more with less

By working at the scale of materials, nanotechnology makes it possible to produce more with less: fewer inputs, less energy, less waste.

Lower impact, longer service life

This translates into more efficient processes, lower environmental impact and solutions with a longer service life, core pillars of the ESG agenda.

Governance and innovation

Beyond the environmental impact, there are direct gains in governance and innovation, with technologies that make production chains smarter and more responsible.

In practice, nanotechnology helps companies align industrial performance with sustainability commitments, without giving up competitiveness.
