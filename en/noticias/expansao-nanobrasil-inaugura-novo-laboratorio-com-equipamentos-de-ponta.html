Expansion: NanoBrasil opens a new laboratory with cutting-edge equipment

Apr 17, 2026·NanoBrasil News Agency·2 min read

More space, more safety, cutting-edge equipment and a structure suited to meeting the increased demand of NanoBrasil's new phase: developing nanoscale solutions on demand for industry.

The laboratory, located at the Belo Horizonte Technology Park (BH-TEC), is part of a strategic phase for the company, which is already aiming for GLP (Good Laboratory Practice) accreditation and accreditation from Inmetro (Brazil's National Institute of Metrology, Quality and Technology).

“We had a very simple laboratory to meet more routine internal needs. We realized that, besides specializing our activities further, we needed accreditation, which we should obtain by the end of this year,” says CEO and co-founder Lucio Coelho.

New capacity

The new facility has doubled in size to 40 square meters and should soon reach 60 square meters. It underwent a complete overhaul to meet NanoBrasil's current and future needs.

“We have partnerships with laboratories, mainly here at UFMG, especially because of the geographical and institutional proximity provided by BH-TEC. But we also needed this increase in capacity in-house,” Coelho adds.

The new equipment includes a high-performance room exhaust system, which ensures safety when working with nanoparticles, and a fume hood, responsible for extracting toxic vapors and protecting professionals during more sensitive handling.

“We acquired some equipment that isn't so common in company laboratories, usually found in more structured laboratories. We have high-precision ovens; pilot-test mixers, which are much more powerful; a V-type powder mixer, among others,” lists the CEO.

The new powder mixer

The powder mixing machine is NanoBrasil's newest acquisition and will be used in projects in the areas of soils and concrete – segments gaining strength in the company's portfolio.

“Until now, almost all our solutions were liquid. We identified that part of the market, especially mining and agribusiness, is demanding dry products and solutions,” explains NanoBrasil's commercial director and co-founder, Oscar Geigner.

“With this state-of-the-art mixer, we have upgraded to the best there is for developing dry solutions. Both for agribusiness, especially poultry litter, and for mining,” he adds.

The new mixer enables perfect homogenization of nanoparticles with each other or with the client's base. In other words, the industry shares a problem or a point to be improved, and NanoBrasil's team of scientists develops a nanoparticle solution based on the base already used by the interested company.

“It has high power compared with mixing by hand, and it allows processes to be standardized, which has a positive impact on the final result of the activity,” explains NanoBrasil laboratory analyst Mariana Magalhães.

The equipment arrived at the laboratory this month and has a speed range of up to 35 RPM, as well as a timer to program how long the nanoparticles need to be mixed.

“You add the desired amount of nanoparticles, whether copper, aluminum or any other material. The mixer ensures homogeneity and the automation mentioned, such as time and power,” the researcher adds.
