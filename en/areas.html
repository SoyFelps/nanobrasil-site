Health and microbial control

Poultry farming

One of NanoBrasil's main focuses in Brazil: bringing antimicrobial nanoparticles to the poultry sector.

Nanocopper and zinc oxide nanoparticles

Poultry litter gathers moisture, organic matter and microorganisms throughout the whole rearing cycle, and it is one of the main sources of flock contamination. Copper nanoparticles act as antimicrobial agents: in contact with bacteria and fungi, they damage their structures and reduce the microbial load of the environment. Because they are much smaller, they have more contact area and can work with a smaller amount of active ingredient.

Scientific research also points to metallic nanoparticles as a promising alternative for poultry production, at a time when the industry seeks to use fewer antibiotics and is concerned about microbial resistance. NanoBrasil develops and supplies these stabilized, standardized nanoparticles, ready to be incorporated into products for the sector.

Paints and coatings

Paints

One of NanoBrasil's main focuses in Brazil: nanoparticles for higher-performance paints and coatings.

Titanium dioxide nanoparticles and calcium carbonate nanoparticles

Nanotechnology applied to paints and coatings uses copper and zinc oxide nanoparticles as antimicrobial agents, inhibiting bacteria and fungi and ensuring safer, more durable surfaces. In addition, titanium dioxide nanoparticles help improve coatings and can replace silver in different applications, reducing costs and increasing the durability and performance of paints, especially powder coatings.

In paints and coatings, performance depends on how the particle is distributed in the formulation. At the nanoscale, particles have much more contact area with the resin, which favors dispersion, finish and the action of the active ingredient on the surface. Titanium dioxide is known for protecting against UV radiation and, under light, can have photocatalytic action, which contributes to self-cleaning surfaces and to the inhibition of microorganisms. Nanoscale calcium carbonate acts as a functional filler, improving properties such as resistance and finish.

Crop protection and nutrition

Agribusiness

Nanocopper and zinc oxide nanoparticles

Agro-industrial nanotechnology uses nanocopper (99.97% metallic) as an effective crop protection agent, with 700x stronger action against pathogens. NanoBrasil integrates nanostructured nutrients: zinc oxide, silicon dioxide, selenium and eco-friendly calcium carbonate, as well as nanoscale boric acid and phosphates, which optimize plant absorption. These solutions increase productivity and reduce losses, ensuring fast protection and efficient nutrition, outperforming conventional methods thanks to the small particle size.

  • Greater efficiency in crop protectionControlled release of active ingredients, reducing losses and increasing effectiveness in the field
  • More efficient plant nutritionNanoscale nutrients that optimize absorption and reduce losses

Home, personal care and pharma

Home care

Nanocopper, nanozinc, nanotitanium

Nanotechnology offers multifaceted solutions: copper nanoparticles act as biocides in household products, providing long-lasting disinfection and sterilization, while zinc oxide and titanium dioxide nanoparticles ensure safe sun protection in cosmetics. In dermocosmetics, copper nanoparticles stimulate collagen and elastin production, boosting anti-wrinkle and healing products. In pharmaceuticals, nanotechnology additives increase absorption and reduce digestive losses. These innovations combine efficiency, safety and long-lasting action, expanding the possibilities in home, personal and therapeutic care with nanometric precision.

  • Advanced antimicrobial actionFewer microorganisms on everyday surfaces and materials
  • Technology applied to healingFilms and patches that help skin regenerate more efficiently

Tough, flame-retardant plastics

Polymers

Calcium carbonate, silicon and titanium dioxide nanoparticles

In polymers, calcium carbonate nanoparticles are used to increase the mechanical strength of packaging, making it possible to use less raw material to achieve the same performance. In addition, silicon and titanium dioxide nanoparticles act as additives with flame-retardant potential, contributing to safer, better-performing materials.

  • Stronger, safer packagingImproved mechanical properties and better product protection
  • Flame-retardant propertiesLower flammability, making materials safer

Concrete and cement strength

Construction

Aluminum dioxide and silicon nanoparticles

Eco-friendly calcium carbonate, titanium dioxide and zinc oxide nanoparticles enhance mechanical properties, such as resistance to abrasion, compression, impact, bending and tension, as well as material durability, and are used in the production of cementitious compounds and high-performance alkali-activated concrete.

  • Greater structural strengthReinforcement for cement and concrete, increasing durability and performance
  • Lower environmental impactMaterial optimization that helps reduce CO₂ emissions

Copper and zinc oxide nanoparticles

Copper and zinc oxide nanoparticles can be incorporated into natural or synthetic fabrics, giving them antimicrobial properties against bacteria and fungi, and insecticidal properties against mites and small insects, acting through oxidative stress and damage to the cell structures of these organisms.

Cat litter and animal products

Pet

Copper and zinc oxide nanoparticles

Nanotechnology applied to the pet segment enables significant advances in odor control and environmental hygiene. Copper and zinc oxide nanoparticles act directly to inhibit the bacteria and fungi that cause bad odors, extending the life of cat litter and reducing the need for frequent changes. These technologies also contribute to safer, healthier environments for animals, with continuous action and longer-lasting products, raising the quality standard of everyday care.

Wound dressings and antiseptics

Health

Copper and zinc oxide nanoparticles

In healthcare, nanotechnology enhances the performance of wound dressings and antiseptic solutions through the antimicrobial action of copper and zinc oxide nanoparticles. These materials help control microorganisms, helping prevent infections and creating a more favorable environment for healing. Working at the nanoscale allows greater contact with the skin surface and efficient release of active ingredients, resulting in products that are more effective, safer and aligned with the demand for innovation in healthcare.
